Tuning an engine, honestly

A small research project. It takes a trading method that has already shown promise and fine-tunes it, one part at a time, before any real money is at risk.

Warren designed an Ichimoku and Fibonacci method from his own trading ideas, and a computer replay of it on past prices showed enough promise to tune it further.

Why we do this

We think a good idea deserves an honest test, not hope. Think of a Formula 1 team: the car is already designed and has already run fast laps. The work now is research and development, tuning each part and proving it holds, before it ever races.

How we work

Rules first. Every test is written down before it runs and is never changed after seeing the result.

Unseen data decides. Settings are chosen on older prices and then checked on newer prices they've never seen.

No real money yet. Everything happens in simulation or on a demo (practice) account. The method stays private while it's tested.

What it has shown so far

SIMULATION ON PAST PRICES · NO MONEY INVOLVED

In the study run of 13 Oct 2026, the shared test account across three markets grew on prices the method had never seen, with deep dips along the way. A smaller test account showed the same shape.

Scored only on the most recent 40% of the recorded prices, which weren't used to pick the settings. Many other settings in the same study lost, which is exactly why the tuning continues.

These are past results from a replay, not live trading. The method is unproven on real money. Testing on a demo (practice) account comes next. Markets change, and real money can be lost, including all of it.

What comes next

Finish the tuning, confirm it once on prices the method has never seen, then run it on a demo account. Real money only after all of that holds.
