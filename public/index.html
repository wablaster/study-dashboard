Tuning an engine, honestly

A small research project. It takes a trading method that has already shown promise and fine-tunes it, one part at a time, before any real money is at risk.

Warren designed an Ichimoku and Fibonacci method from his own trading ideas, and a computer replay of it on past prices grew $1,000 to about $3,131.

Why we do this

We think a good idea deserves an honest test, not hope. Think of a Formula 1 team: the car is already designed and has already run fast laps. The work now is research and development, tuning each part and proving it holds, before it ever races.

How we work

Rules first. Every test is written down before it runs and is never changed after seeing the result.

Unseen data decides. Settings are chosen on older prices and then checked on newer prices they've never seen.

No real money yet. Everything happens in simulation or on a demo (practice) account. The method stays private while it's tested.

What it has shown so far

SIMULATION ON PAST PRICES · NO MONEY INVOLVED $1,000 → $3,130.98

The account doubled after about 5 days of replayed market time. Along the way it fell as much as 30% below its high point.

Study run of 13 Oct 2026. One shared test account across three markets, a fixed $20 stake per trade, scored only on the most recent 40% of the recorded prices, which weren't used to pick the settings.
SIMULATION ON PAST PRICES · NO MONEY INVOLVED $100 → $206.55

The same test with a small account doubled after about 7 days, with a worst dip of 15.6%.

Same run and the same unseen window. Many other settings in the same study lost money, which is exactly why the tuning continues.

These are past results from a replay, not live trading. The method is unproven on real money. Testing on a demo (practice) account comes next.
